Four kinds of evidence

A number

From a study — most people do not post their down moments.

A quotation

What an expert said — Rio: “It's not the reality.”

A public example

Everyone can see it — a famous person's photo that is clearly edited.

A private example

Only you saw it — “my friend was sad after reading the comments.”
