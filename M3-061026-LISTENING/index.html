Track — the seven questions

The missing words

1. What is the report trying to explain?

2. PISA is an IQ test.

3. Using screens for fun at school improves your scores.

4. In which subject is the drop steepest?

5. What is the fall in reading equivalent to?

6. Why doesn't banning phones at school solve the problem?

7. Does the report say this is a problem only for teenagers?

Answer the questions

Use your notes to answer the seven questions.

The answers

1. Why scores are falling — and it is not one cause.

2. False — it tests real-world problem-solving in science, maths and reading.

3. False — screens for fun (social media, games) meant lower scores.

4. Reading — one of the biggest falls the researchers have seen.

5. A whole year of school.

6. Because devices are everywhere at home, not only at school.

7. No — other generations are struggling to focus too.
