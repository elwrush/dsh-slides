In Thai, we nod

... and other things that don’t work in English

Four Thai habits

เกรงใจ
You don’t want to make trouble for them. So you don’t say what you really think.
เสียหน้า
It is bad to be wrong in front of people. So you don’t give a strong opinion.
ใจเย็น
You must stay calm. So you stop the argument, or you make it softer.
อายุ
You don’t say an older person is wrong. Not in front of other people.

A Thai nod means “I have heard you”. It does not mean “I agree”.

Four rules for this room

Disagreeing is good. If nobody answers, it looks like nobody is listening.
The position is not the person. You can say the position is wrong. You never say the person is wrong.
An opinion needs a because. Without one, you have not finished.
The ending is one sentence you both say. Not silence. Not the bell.

Five problems

Five problems, five suggestions

The problemThe suggestion
Turn-takingOne talks, the other waits.
Take short turns. End yours with a question: “What do you think?”
“I agree, I agree”Four times in a minute.
No “I agree” in the first thirty seconds.
Not listeningThe next line is ready while they talk.
Repeat a word or a phrase of theirs before you answer. “Free time?” — Yes, I think we need more of that.
No evidenceThe opinion stands alone.
Two kinds of evidence. Only one can be about someone you know. A number, a quote, or a public example — not only a friend’s story.
No ending“OK. OK, OK.”
Last thirty seconds: “So our answer is…”

Phrases you can use

Question 1 of 6 · Position

Your partner says nothing. You need to start.

AWhere did you read that?

BSo our answer is that they try.

CFor me, the club does help.

DBut that is only one teacher, right?

Question 2 of 6 · Reason

You have said what you think. Now you must say why.

AThat is a good point.

BI don’t remember where I read it.

C…because nobody goes to the guidance teacher.

DMm. Agreed.

Question 3 of 6 · Evidence

Your partner does not believe you. You need to show how you know.

AMy cousin told her teacher, and the teacher said to ignore it.

BI see what you mean.

CWhat do you think?

DSo we agree, then.

Question 4 of 6 · Test

Your partner gives a number, and you are not sure about it.

AWhere did you read that?

BSo our answer is…

COK, that is true.

DI think it too.

Question 5 of 6 · Concede

Your partner says something you think is true.

ADo you know that, or do you think it?

BI read that somewhere.

CI don’t agree.

DThat is a good point.

Question 6 of 6 · Close

The two minutes are nearly over. You must finish together.

AWhere did you read that?

BSo our answer is: they try, but they start too late.

CMy cousin told me.

DHmm, I don’t agree.
