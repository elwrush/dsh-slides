Let's look at the Spider-Man narrative arc

The arc in Spider-Man

Exposition He is a shy senior in Queens, and Mary Jane has never spoken to him.
Inciting incident A spider in the genetics laboratory bites his hand.
Rising action Uncle Ben is shot, and the Goblin asks Peter to join him.
Climax The Goblin hangs Mary Jane and a tram full of children over the bridge, and makes Peter choose one.
Falling action The Goblin dies on his own glider, and his son Harry blames Spider-Man.
Resolution He tells Mary Jane he can only be her friend, and walks away.

The words the narrator uses

Listen

Exposition Peter Parker is seventeen. He lives in Queens with his aunt and uncle. He is clever, and he is not popular. Mary Jane, the girl in his class, has never spoken to him.
Inciting incident On a school trip to a genetics laboratory, a spider drops onto his hand and bites it. He goes home sick, and in the morning his glasses are useless: he can see the clock across the room.
Rising action Peter lets a thief run past him with the takings, and that night the same thief shoots Uncle Ben in the street. Norman Osborn, who owns the laboratory, tests his own experiment on himself, becomes the Green Goblin, and asks Peter to join him.
Climax On the bridge the Goblin cuts the cable and gives Peter a choice: Mary Jane, or the tram full of children. Peter catches them both.
Falling action In a warehouse the Goblin beats him and calls the glider to finish him. Peter jumps aside, and the glider kills the Goblin instead. Harry, the Goblin's son, arrives to see Spider-Man standing over his father's body.
Resolution At the funeral Peter tells Mary Jane that he can only ever be her friend. Then he walks away.

Exposition

The world before the trouble starts.

He is a shy senior at a Queens high school, and Mary Jane has never spoken to him.

Who the story is about.

Where and when it happens.

What is normal for them.

In your notebook · your story

1Draw one picture of your exposition.

2Write two sentences: what is normal.

Inciting incident

The thing that breaks the normal world.

On a school trip, a spider in the genetics laboratory bites his hand.

It is one event, on one day.

It happens to your main character.

After it, they cannot go back.

In your notebook · your story

1Draw the moment it happens.

2Write two sentences: what has changed?

Rising action

The trouble gets worse, step by step.

Uncle Ben is shot, and the Goblin asks Peter to join him.

Two or three things go wrong.

Each one narrows the choice.

The last one is the worst.

In your notebook · your story

1Draw one of the steps.

2Write two sentences: what is going wrong?

Climax

The moment your character cannot avoid.

The Goblin hangs Mary Jane and a tram full of children over the bridge, and makes Peter choose one.

The highest point of trouble.

A decision, or a discovery.

It cannot be undone.

In your notebook · your story

1Draw the climax.

2Write two sentences: what do they do?

Falling action

What happens because of the climax.

The Goblin dies on his own glider, and his son Harry blames Spider-Man.

The consequences arrive.

Loose ends are closed.

The tension goes out of it.

In your notebook · your story

1Draw the falling action.

2Write two sentences: what is the result?

Resolution

The world after the trouble is over.

He tells Mary Jane he can only be her friend, and walks away.

The trouble is finished.

What is different now.

One line is enough.
