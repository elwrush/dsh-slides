Let's look at the Cinderella narrative arc

The arc in Cinderella

Exposition She works for her stepmother and stepsisters.
Inciting incident An invitation to the ball arrives.
Rising action Her fairy godmother sends her to the ball, with a warning.
Climax At midnight she runs, and loses a slipper.
Falling action The prince searches with the slipper.
Resolution The slipper fits. They marry.

The words she uses

Listen

Exposition Cinderella's mother died. Her father married again, and the new wife had two daughters of her own. They took the house for themselves and gave Cinderella the work: the fire, the floors, the washing. She slept in the kitchen, in the ashes.
Inciting incident One morning an invitation came to the door. The king was giving a ball, and every young woman in the kingdom was asked to come. Her sisters read it twice, and told her she could not.
Rising action She was still in the kitchen that evening when a woman stood in front of her, in grey light. The woman gave her a coach, a dress, and one warning. Leave before midnight. Cinderella went, and she danced with the prince, and she forgot the warning.
Climax Then the clock began to strike. She ran, and one glass slipper stayed behind on the step.
Falling action The next day the prince walked the kingdom with the slipper in his hand. Every woman in the country tried it on. At Cinderella's house her sisters pushed her aside and tried first. Neither of them could get a foot into it.
Resolution Then the slipper went on Cinderella. They were married in the spring.

Exposition

The world before the trouble starts.

In Cinderella: she works for her stepmother and her two stepsisters.

Who the story is about.

Where and when it happens.

What is normal for them.

Inciting incident

The thing that breaks the normal world.

In Cinderella: an invitation to the ball arrives.

It is one event, on one day.

It happens to your main character.

After it, they cannot go back.

In your notebook · your story

1Draw the moment it happens.

2Write two sentences: what has changed?

Rising action

The trouble gets worse, step by step.

In Cinderella: the godmother sends her to the ball, but she must leave by midnight.

Two or three things go wrong.

Each one narrows the choice.

The last one is the worst.

In your notebook · your story

1Draw one of the steps.

2Write two sentences: what is going wrong?

Climax

The moment your character cannot avoid.

In Cinderella: the clock strikes twelve and she runs, because the magic ends.

The highest point of trouble.

A decision, or a discovery.

It cannot be undone.

In your notebook · your story

1Draw the climax.

2Write two sentences: what do they do?

Falling action

What happens because of the climax.

In Cinderella: the prince searches with the slipper, because she left it behind.

The consequences arrive.

Loose ends are closed.

The tension goes out of it.

In your notebook · your story

1Draw the falling action.

2Write two sentences: what is the result?

Resolution

The world after the trouble is over.

In Cinderella: the slipper fits, and they marry.

The trouble is finished.

What is different now.

One line is enough.
