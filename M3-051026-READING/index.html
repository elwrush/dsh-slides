But first

Let’s remember how to read together.

There are 4 jobs in each group.

Predictor Clarifier Questioner Summariser

First job

The Predictor

Your job is to read the headline and the image, and guess what the chunk is about. Then tell your group — like this:

Dialog 1 — the Predictor

AvaI’m the Predictor, and the heading says “Lateness Costs Learning Time.” I haven’t read it yet, so this is a real guess. I think it’s going to say you miss the beginning of the lesson, and the first minutes matter most, because that’s when the teacher explains what to do.

MarcusI agree, and I can see it in the title — it asks if being on time affects your grades, so Section 1 has to say yes first. But it goes further: if somebody walks in late, the teacher stops and repeats everything, so the class loses time too. That’s why it says costs.

AvaRight. And I think it’ll give the habit a name — probably tardiness, the word in the glossary.

MarcusLet’s read it and see if you’re right.

Second job

The Clarifier

Your job is to find the words and sentences that stop you, and work out what they mean. Then tell your group — like this:

Dialog 2 — the Clarifier

MarcusI’m the Clarifier, so I’ll go first. This bit stopped me: “students who arrive late start the day behind.” Behind what? There’s nothing behind them.

AvaI think it means behind in the work. The sentence before says the others are ready to start learning.

MarcusYes — so it’s not about where you are. It’s about where you are in the lesson. That’s what makes it a problem.

AvaAnd the next sentence shows why. The lesson has already started without you.

Third job

The Questioner

Your job is to ask a question the chunk has to answer — and notice what it still does not say. Then tell your group — like this:

Dialog 3 — the Questioner

PriyaI’m the Questioner. What does the late student actually lose, and can they get it back?

AvaThe text answers the first part. They miss the announcements, the instructions, and the first explanations of new work.

PriyaAnd it never says anyone gives it back. It just adds the minutes up.

AvaSo the answer is bigger than the question. The text says the whole class loses time too — so it isn’t only the late student’s problem.

Fourth job

The Summariser

Your job is to put the chunk into one sentence, in as few words as you can. Then tell your group — like this:

Dialog 4 — the Summariser

DevonI’m the Summariser. One sentence for the whole section: small bits of lateness add up to a lot of lost teaching, and the class pays too, not only the student who was late.

MarcusThat’s the section, in half the words. But it leaves out the word the text uses for the habit.

DevonTardiness. So — the text calls the habit tardiness, and says it costs real learning time: the late student’s and the class’s.

MarcusNow it’s a summary I could carry into the next chunk. That’s the point of it.
