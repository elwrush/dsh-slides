The six moves, in order

1

PositionYour answer to the question.

2

ReasonWhy. The because.

3

EvidenceHow you know.

4

TestHow do they know?

5

ConcedeAgree when you hear truth.

6

CloseOne answer, in new words.

Out loud

Say all six, in order, in one minute. Then say the one you never do.

The echo

What they said

Example 1

Student 1: You know, I really think homework is a waste of time.

Student 2: A waste of time, huh. Yes, I’d have to agree with that. I usually just copy off my friends, and it doesn’t have any effect on my grades.

Example 2

Student 1: I really get annoyed with how teachers behave towards girls. They are so much stricter with girls than boys.

Student 2: You’re saying that teachers are harder on girls than boys! No way! Boys in my class always get called nitwit, but girls never do.

Listen and write

You will hear the tape twice. Don’t write anything the first time. Write down any useful language after the tape has been played a second time.

Two ways to echo

1 · The same words

A waste of time, huh. Yes, and [your reason].

2 · Your own words

So you’re saying [their point]. I don’t think so, because [your reason].

Out loud

Say the echo before you answer. Three of their words is enough, or the same idea in your words.

Your own card

The recorded question

Online safety needs to be taught by schools.

Two minutes. Random pairs. I record it. One take. Twenty marks.

Choose a side

Card A · For

Schools should teach it. They have every child in a class.

Card B · Against

Schools are the wrong people to teach it. It has to come from a parent.

Take a card

Take the side you believe. Write only your own side, in your own words. Eight boxes.

At the recording I draw the side, and about half of you will be moved. You have already argued both sides five times today.

What goes in each box

1

PositionSchools should… or schools should not…

2

ReasonBecause… One sentence you believe.

3

EvidenceOne number, one quote, one example.

4

TestThe question you will ask. Word for word.

5

ConcedeThe thing you will accept from them.

6

CloseSo our answer is…

On your card

Eight boxes. Keep each line short enough to say without reading it.
