Suggested answers

1

The PISA test is taken every three years.

Every three years, in 91 countries and regions.

2

The steepest drop was in reading.

Reading — one of the biggest falls the researchers have seen.

3

The reading fall equals a whole year of school.

A whole year of school, gone.

4

Students go home to a world where phones are always available.

1 Position

I absolutely agree with that.

your partner speaks

2 Reason

3 Evidence

your partner speaks

4 Rebutand debate

So you're saying social media has nothing to do with it?

5 Concede

6 Validate
