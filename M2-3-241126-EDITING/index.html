What's the best thing about this writing?

Write it down

Read the story and write down the highlight.

Example: I loved the way you write about the fear experienced by Tee, the brave, tall, handsome hero. When you wrote “Tee’s heart was pounding in his chest as he rounded the corner and saw Plearn, the savage monster,” I almost clapped.

Pass your writing and editing sheet to the person on the left.

What improvement can you suggest?

Write one suggestion

Maybe the story is a bit confusing, or it could be a little more exciting.

For example: when your hero fights the enemy, it would be great if they yelled “Shazam!”

Grammar

The five most common mistakes

In Pencil

Circle and correct any mistake you see.

Missing capital letters — i → I

Bad punctuation — missing commas and full stops, wrong spacing: He is 16 .He is happy → He is 16. He is happy.

Subject–verb agreement — She love to go to the park → She loves to go to the park.

Tense — She go to America last year → She went to America last year.

Spelling — pround → proud
