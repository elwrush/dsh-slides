What do you know about cryptocurrency?

Before we read

Three things to know

  • Cryptocurrency is digital money. There is no coin in your pocket.
  • A wallet holds the keys that prove the money is yours. Lose the keys, lose the money.
  • An exchange is a company where people buy and sell it. Binance is one.
  • About US$6.3 billion was stolen — roughly 211,000 ล้านบาท.

Three things that help

Questions the text never answers (inferences)

A worked example — not from your text

The shop on the corner

Mr Korn’s shop opens at seven every morning. On Tuesday its shutters were still down at nine, and the sign above the door was gone. His brother was working at the stall two doors along.

What has happened to Mr Korn’s shop?

In your notebook

Answer the question. Then write the three things in the text that made you sure.

How that was done

The shop has closed

The shutters are still down at nine.

The sign above the door is gone.

His brother is working somewhere else.

So: the shop has closed — and the text never says so. You say it.

The method

Four steps

1

Find what the text does say.

2

Ask what those facts add up to.

3

Answer with the conclusion and the clues you used.

4

Do not add a fact that is not there.
