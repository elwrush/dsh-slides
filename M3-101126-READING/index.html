What do you know about cryptocurrency?

Before we read

Three things to know

  • Cryptocurrency is digital money. There is no coin in your pocket.
  • A wallet holds the keys that prove the money is yours. Lose the keys, lose the money.
  • An exchange is a company where people buy and sell it. Binance is one.
  • In this theft, US$6.3 billion went — about 211,000 ล้านบาท.

Question 1 of 4

What is cryptocurrency?

A Money that exists only on computers.

B Paper money you can hold.

C A kind of bank account.

D A company that lends money.

A. It is digital money — nothing physical changes hands.

Question 2 of 4

What does a crypto-wallet actually hold?

A The coins themselves.

B The keys that prove the money is yours.

C Your bank account number.

D A list of everything you bought.

B. The money is on the blockchain. The wallet holds the keys to it.

Question 3 of 4

What is an exchange like Binance for?

A Sending letters.

B Keeping paper money safe.

C Buying and selling cryptocurrency.

D Insuring a mobile phone.

C. An exchange is a company where customers buy and sell digital money.

Question 4 of 4

Why is a stolen laptop dangerous for someone who owns cryptocurrency?

A It is expensive to replace.

B It holds their holiday photographs.

C It uses a lot of electricity.

D The keys to the money may be on it.

D. A password or a key on the machine can open the money.

Questions the text never answers

A worked example — not from your text

The shop on the corner

Mr Somchai’s shop opens at seven every morning. On Tuesday its shutters were still down at nine, and the sign above the door was gone. His brother was working at the stall two doors along.

What has happened to Mr Somchai’s shop?

In your notebook

Answer the question. Then write the three things in the text that made you sure.

How that was done

The shop has closed

The shutters are still down at nine.

The sign above the door is gone.

His brother is working somewhere else.

So: the shop has closed — and the text never says so. You say it.
