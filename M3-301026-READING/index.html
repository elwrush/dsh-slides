Your roles · 1 of 4

Predictor

Before each chunk. It wakes up what you already know about the subject, and it gives you something to check yourself against.

Out loud — before each chunk

1Look at the heading and the picture. Use what you already know about the subject.

2Say what you expect this chunk to be about, and why you think so.

3After you read, check: were you right?

Your roles · 2 of 4

Summariser

After each chunk. In your own words — a summary is not the sentences said again.

Out loud — after each chunk

1One or two sentences: what was the main idea of this chunk?

2The group says what you left out. That is part of the job, not a criticism.

Your roles · 3 of 4

Clarifier

Any time something stops making sense. A word, a phrase you cannot follow, or an idea that does not fit.

Out loud — the moment it happens

1Say exactly what you did not understand, and point at the words.

2The group answers. If nobody can, bring it to me.

3Then say it back in your own words, so you know it is fixed.

Your roles · 4 of 4

Questioner

After each chunk. Your questions are the test the group has to pass, and they tell you whether you really understood it.

Out loud — after each chunk

1Ask two questions about the main ideas — not about small details.

2The group answers, and says whether the answer is complete.

3Then one question the text does not answer: what do you still want to know?
